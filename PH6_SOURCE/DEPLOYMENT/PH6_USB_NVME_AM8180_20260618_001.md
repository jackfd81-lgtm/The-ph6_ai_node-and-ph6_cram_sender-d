## U1/U2 LINK POWER MANAGEMENT MESSAGES

Observed at enumeration:
```
usb 4-1: Enable of device-initiated U1 failed.  (×2)
usb 4-1: Enable of device-initiated U2 failed.  (×2)
```

**Classification: Non-blocking cosmetic enumeration warnings.**

These are USB 3.0 LPM (Link Power Management) state negotiation failures.
The AM8180 bridge does not support device-initiated U1/U2 power states.
Messages occur once at connect-time only. No resets, no I/O failures, no
disconnects, and no UAS aborts follow. This is known AM8180 behaviour.

These are NOT:
- `reset SuperSpeed USB device`
- `uas_eh_abort_handler`
- `I/O error`
- `Buffer I/O error`
- `device disconnected`

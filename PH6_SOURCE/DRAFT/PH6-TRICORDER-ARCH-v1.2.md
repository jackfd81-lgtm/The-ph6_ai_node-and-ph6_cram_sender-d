## 16. Tricorder Identity & Deterministic Evidence Requirement

### 16.0 System Definition

PH6 is a deterministic scientific evidence instrument operating in a
tricorder configuration, with bounded Lane-2 AI assisting interpretation.

PH6 is not designed to function as a conventional chatbot, agent framework,
automation platform, or evidence archive.

PH6 is a scientific instrument architecture whose purpose is to:

- observe reality,
- measure reality,
- validate measurements,
- preserve evidence,
- model environments,
- maintain continuity through time,
- support environmental understanding,
- assist human operators with bounded AI cognition.

The PH6 objective is environmental understanding grounded in preserved evidence.

Environmental understanding is a first-class system objective.

### 16.1 Tricorder Doctrine

The PH6 tricorder mission is:

```text
Reality
  -> Observation
  -> Measurement
  -> Validation
  -> Preservation
  -> Environmental Modeling
  -> Continuity
  -> Cognitive Observability
  -> Understanding
```

Each stage depends upon the integrity of the stages before it.

Understanding without preserved evidence is prohibited.

Environmental models without measurable observations are advisory only.

AI conclusions without preserved evidence must never be treated as authoritative.

This mission chain operates within, and does not replace, the Master
Governing Seal: CRAM preserves -> PSEUDO measures -> PSEUDO-A adjudicates ->
Certification proves -> SoSo observes -> AI advises -> Book V explores ->
Lane 1 decides.

### 16.2 Deterministic Evidence Requirement

Deterministic evidence is a constitutional requirement of PH6.

Claims must be supported by preserved observations whenever practical.

System reports should prefer evidence over assertion.

The preferred order is:

```text
Observation -> Measurement -> Validation -> Preservation -> Interpretation
```

Interpretation must never replace preserved evidence.

When reporting system status, testing results, or environmental findings,
do not report only success or failure. Report:

- what was observed,
- what was measured,
- what was preserved,
- what was validated,
- what was inferred,
- what remains unknown.

Evidence must be distinguishable from interpretation.

Placement note: this evidence-first requirement is PROPOSED pending explicit
Book I / Book II / Book VI integration and is not yet independently ratified
as constitutional doctrine.

### 16.3 AI Mission Role

AI is a required component of the PH6 tricorder mission.

AI exists to:

- interpret preserved observations,
- build environmental models,
- identify continuity relationships,
- assist discovery,
- generate hypotheses,
- compare observations across time,
- support operator understanding.

AI is not authorized to:

- redefine evidence,
- alter preserved observations,
- issue PASS/DROP authority,
- modify deterministic adjudication.

AI capability is mission-critical. AI authority remains bounded.

Division of labor: SoSo performs topology, continuity, drift, and
token-relationship observation as Authority ZERO; AI may assist with
interpretation, hypothesis generation, summarization, and operator-facing
explanation as Authority ZERO. Neither may issue PASS/DROP, redefine
evidence, alter preserved observations, or modify deterministic adjudication.

### 16.4 Guiding Principle — PROPOSED

The purpose of PH6 is not merely to know what happened.

The purpose of PH6 is to understand what is happening, what changed, what
remains connected, and why — while preserving the evidence required to
verify those conclusions.

### 16.5 Operational Rule (Claude Code / Lane-2 AI)

```text
When evaluating PH6 tests, prioritize deterministic evidence over
narrative summaries.

A test report is incomplete if it contains conclusions without
accompanying evidence artifacts, measurements, hashes, metrics,
topology outputs, token outputs, or preserved observations.
```
